JSON includes statistics, observations and chart samples. CSV contains the reduced chart samples, not every raw sample.

Raw status and printer configuration are never exported. Empty or capped windows cannot be exported as complete reports.

Coverage, interpretation, and capture limits

Sampling can miss events between polls. No encoder position, mechanical torque, or numeric motor temperature is provided. A flag that disappears after missing data is not labelled a confirmed recovery.
