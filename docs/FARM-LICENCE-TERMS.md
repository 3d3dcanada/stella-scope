# Stella Scope farm licence terms

These terms apply to a farm licence for Stella Scope sold by 3D3D (Randall Marshall, 3D3D Canada). The
code itself remains AGPL-3.0 public source; a farm licence is a commercial right to run more than five
printers with the official, supported builds, plus support.

## Who needs a farm licence

Stella Scope is free for up to five printers. A farm licence is required from the sixth printer onward in
an official build. Demo printers (simulations running on the person's own machine) never count toward the
five.

## What a licence covers

A licence is issued for a named farm and a number of printers (for example, "Maple Street Print Farm, 12
printers"). It covers running that many printers with the official Stella Scope builds. One licence covers
one farm; it is not transferable to another farm or person without 3D3D's written agreement.

## Perpetual or yearly

A farm licence is perpetual: once issued for a printer count, it does not expire and covers that count of
printers indefinitely. There is no yearly fee. 3D3D may offer a separate support subscription, but support
is not required to keep using a licence.

## Updates

A licence covers the Stella Scope version current when it is issued and subsequent minor updates. A major
release may require a new licence at 3D3D's then-current price; 3D3D will say so before the release.

## Transfer and refunds

A licence is not transferable. Payments are final; there are no refunds. If a printer count was ordered in
error, contact info@3d3d.ca before paying and 3D3D will correct the order.

## Support

Support (installation help, licence re-issuing if the count changes within the same farm, troubleshooting
official builds) is provided by email to info@3d3d.ca during 3D3D's normal business hours. Support does not
cover printers built from source, modified builds, or hardware faults.

## No warranty

Stella Scope and the official builds are provided "as is", without warranty of any kind, to the extent
permitted by law. 3D3D is not liable for failed prints, downtime, or any indirect loss.

## How to buy

1. Write to info@3d3d.ca with your farm name and the number of printers you want licensed (for example,
   "Maple Street Print Farm, 12 printers"). 3D3D replies with an invoice.
2. Pay the invoice by Interac e-Transfer. The payment details come with the invoice.
3. 3D3D issues the licence within one business day of payment and emails it to you. It is one line of text
   beginning `SFL1.`
4. Paste it into Stella Scope's Farm page ("Enter licence"). Nothing is sent anywhere; the licence is
   checked offline on your computer.
