This model of the Original Prusa i3 MK3S+ (and the MK3S and MK3, which share its frame, axes and plate) ships inside Stella Scope. This is its notice: who made it, the terms it is under and what was done to the file. It is also shown under About and under Printer models.

WHO MADE IT

Swesen made a Fusion 360 model from that assembly: "Original Prusa i3 MK3 for Fusion 360 (and STL)", Printables model 179638, CC BY. Swesen rebuilt the frame and the Y carriage from Prusa's DXF files, remade the E3D V6 from the official drawings, and organised and named the parts.

The MK3 model is a drawing of the MK3 as it was published; the MK3S+ has the same frame, axes and plate, and its IR sensor and filament sensor are small parts the model shows as the MK3's.

WHAT WAS DONE TO IT
The STEP assembly was converted into a triangle mesh in glTF 2.0 binary form (a .glb file, then gzipped) so that Stella Scope can draw the printer on screen. The parts were named and grouped so that the axes can move with the printer (the Y axis assembly's moving components are the bed, the X axis assembly the gantry that climbs, the extruder assembly the head). The colours are the model's own. Nothing was added to the geometry. The conversion is tools/printer-cad/step_to_glb.py in the Stella Scope source, with tools/printer-cad/prusa-mk3s/groups.json; anyone can repeat it from the STEP file and gets the same bytes.
